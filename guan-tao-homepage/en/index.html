Tao Guan 关涛

Aeronautical and Astronautical Science and Technology · Control

I am a Ph.D. student in Aeronautical and Astronautical Science and Technology at Sichuan University, working on intelligent decision-making and control under complex constraints. I expect to graduate in June 2027. I received my master's degree in Electronic Information from Sichuan University in 2023 and my bachelor's degree in Automation from Northeastern University in 2020.

My research interests include constrained nonlinear control, spacecraft attitude control, fixed-time and prescribed-performance control, and distributionally robust model predictive control. My work has appeared in journals including IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control and IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems.
